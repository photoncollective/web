## 2. Business Model & Strategic Direction

**This is the most important section for understanding what Photon Collective is and where it's going.**

### Right now: Consultancy + pre-built products
Photon Collective operates primarily as a **consultancy**. We engage directly with investment managers, understand their workflows, and build custom solutions for them. We have four pre-built platform products (see Section 6) that serve as mature starting points — but these are not sold as off-the-shelf SaaS. They are deployed, customized, and extended as part of a consulting engagement.

The typical path: discovery → custom build or adapted platform product → ongoing refinement.

### Where we're headed: A modular OS for investment managers
The long-term vision is to generalize these individual tools into a **full modular operating system for investment managers** — a suite of composable products that firms can adopt in pieces and assemble into a complete operational stack. Each current product (OMS, stock screening, client onboarding, regulatory intelligence) is a module in that eventual system.

**The distinction matters:**
- Today, a client engages us to solve a specific problem. We consult, build, and customize.
- Eventually, a client will be able to adopt individual modules from a productized platform, still with customization available on top.

We are not there yet. The consultancy model and hands-on engagement are the core of the business right now.

## 7. The Engagement Model

Every engagement starts with one workflow. No large upfront commitments.

1. **Define** — Discovery workshop; map workflows and align on objectives/KPIs
2. **Design** — Tailored workflows and interfaces
3. **Prototype** — Working build on real client data, typically within **2–3 weeks**
4. **Iterate** — Test, refine, align until it fits real use
5. **Structure** — Turn fragmented data into a clean, connected foundation
6. **Expand or close** — Client evaluates at each stage and decides whether to continue

The process is transparent, predictable, and budget-controlled. At close, source code, documentation, and credentials transfer to the client. No vendor lock-in.

## 10. FAQs (Verbatim)

**Q: A new tech initiative feels like a big lift. Why should we choose you?**
> We make it easy to start. You do not need to figure out architecture, data, or which tools to use. Just describe your problem. We structure projects in small, controlled stages so you can see real output early, avoid heavy upfront costs, and de-risk the initiative.

**Q: What does the engagement approach look like in practice?**
> We begin with a small workflow of your choice. First, we review your data and define the exact objective. Next, we build a working prototype that uses your real information. Then we refine it together. After each stage you evaluate progress and decide whether to continue. This makes the process transparent, predictable, and budget-controlled.

**Q: What do you actually build?**
> We build custom internal systems for financial workflows. We connect to the data you already have, normalize it, and use it to streamline review processes and decision-making. The result: less manual repetition, fewer errors, and more efficient use of your time.

**Q: How much change is required on our side?**
> Minimal. We integrate with your current tools and data sources. You do not have to overhaul your systems to begin and we build in parallel to your daily work.

**Q: Is this secure and compliant for regulated financial work?**
> Yes. Your data remains in controlled environments with encrypted transfer, governed access, and full audit logging.

**Q: How long until we can test something live?**
> Typically within 2–3 weeks for an initial workflow, depending on data access and complexity. We prioritize early usability and fast conceptual design.

**Q: Do we have to commit upfront to rebuilding a full system?**
> No. We start with one workflow. If it delivers value, we expand. If not, you stop. Our staged model is specifically meant to avoid large upfront commitments, long cycles, or "big bang" implementations.

**Q: How do you ensure we can trust the AI or any automated process?**
> Every AI suggestion we show is traceable back to its exact original source. Nothing is "black box". We design for visibility first: if a value is surfaced, the user can instantly see the document or dataset it came from. But most importantly, AI in automation is optional. In most workflows, a well-structured interface linked with a database already creates a huge efficiency gain.
